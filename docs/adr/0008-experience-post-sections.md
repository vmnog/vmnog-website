# Experience posts: three fixed headings, short is fine

Status: accepted (2026-11-17, ticket experience-pages/01, A11)

Every post uses the same three headings, in this order:

1. What the company does
2. What I did
3. What I took from it

A role with little to tell (the internships at Commerzbank and Cremesp, Needles under its NDA) may
have one or two sentences under each heading. Short is fine; padding is not.

**Why:** consistent pages, and no filler on thin roles.

**Rejected:** a minimum of three paragraphs per heading (A11-2); free form with no headings (A11-3).
